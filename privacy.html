Privacy (short version)

What I collect

Name, email, and your task description (via the Telegram contact or email). Payment details are handled by Stripe or PayPal and never reach me.

Why

To deliver the work, handle payment, and answer follow-ups.

How long

While the service runs and for the records the law requires; I delete on request.

Who sees it

No one. Your data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Your rights

Ask me at any time what I store about you, and ask for correction or deletion. Contact: via the Telegram link on the site.
