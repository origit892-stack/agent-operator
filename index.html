FAQ

Asked often.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. I need access to the tools you want connected (email, CRM, sheets, WhatsApp) and a description of the task. You get docs and a walkthrough so your team can live with it.

What if the agent makes a mistake?

Guardrails on (stays on topic, refuses to guess, flags low confidence), every action logged, human handoff for anything sensitive. You approve the risky steps.

How does payment work?

50% to start, 50% on delivery, via Stripe or PayPal. Included support days are part of the package — see the terms.

Where are you based?

Israel (UTC+2), working language English, full overlap with the European workday and US Eastern afternoons.
